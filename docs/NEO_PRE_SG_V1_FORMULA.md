# NEO Pre-tournament SG Probability Model V1

## Scope

V1 produces cut, TOP20, TOP10, TOP5, and win probabilities from official
round-level **total Strokes Gained (SG)** known before the target event.
It is implemented in `klpga_pipeline/src/klpga/neo_win/sg_pre_v1.py` and
the S-OIL build is `klpga_pipeline/scripts/276_build_soil_2026100001_pre_sg.py`.

V1 uses total SG only. Total SG already contains tee-to-green and putting
contributions (off-the-tee, approach, around-green, and putting); adding
those components on top of total SG would count the same performance twice.
Component-only alternatives require a separate backtest.

## Player forecast

For player `i` and each strictly prior SG round `j`, let `g_ij` be official
total SG and `d_ij` be the number of days between that round and the target
event's start date. The recency weight is:

`w_ij = 2 ** (-d_ij / 90)`

The expected round SG is shrunk toward the tour reference of zero by 20
equivalent rounds:

`mu_i = sum(w_ij * g_ij) / (sum(w_ij) + 20)`

The pooled round-to-round standard deviation is estimated from prior
player histories. A player's own standard deviation is blended toward the
pooled value using 12 prior degrees of freedom. A player with no SG history
gets a neutral mean of zero and pooled variability for the whole-field
simulation; their individual probability cells display `—` because no
player-specific SG estimate exists.

## Tournament simulation

Each simulation independently draws a round SG for each player from that
player's estimated mean and variability. Higher SG becomes a lower
simulated stroke difference, rounded to a whole stroke. After round two,
the model advances the 60th-place score and everyone tied with it. The
remaining rounds determine final ranks. Ties at TOP20/TOP10/TOP5 thresholds
are included; first-place ties share win credit.

All five probabilities are the frequency of the corresponding result in
the same 60,000 simulations. This makes TOP20 a subset of cut passage,
TOP10 a subset of TOP20, TOP5 a subset of TOP10, and a win a subset of TOP5.
The full field's win probabilities sum to 1.

## S-OIL V1 input coverage

The retained SG source covers 11 2026 events through 2026-10-18: 3,882
player-ID-linked round records, 221 players, and complete total/component
fields. In the 96-player S-OIL entry list, 91 have at least one linked SG
round (2,811 linked rounds); five have no linked player SG history. S-OIL
uses only records dated before 2026-10-29.

## Exploratory historical check

`NEO_PRE_SG_V1_WALK_FORWARD_2026_EXPLORATORY.md` records a chronological
test on ten events with prior SG available. One earlier event was skipped
because no earlier SG event was retained. The SG signal was compared with
a score-only signal through the same shrinkage and tournament simulator.
Across these ten events SG had slightly lower Brier and log-loss values
for cut, TOP20, TOP10, TOP5, and win. The actual winner appeared in the SG
model's top ten in one of ten events. This is a small one-season sample;
it does not establish multi-season calibration or prove that SG is better.

## Limits

- There is no full-field round-level SG archive for prior seasons in the repository.
- Five S-OIL entrants have no player-linked SG record and are included only with a neutral prior so the field remains complete; their public cells remain `—`.
- This candidate has not been validated on the future S-OIL result.
- Event-specific cut terms must be checked against the event regulations before treating the cut probability as final.
- The S-OIL preview generated by this candidate is local and has not been pushed or deployed.
